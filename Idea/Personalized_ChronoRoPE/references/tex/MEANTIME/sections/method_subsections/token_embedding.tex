We keep a learnable embedding matrix $\mathbf{M}^I \in \mathbb{R}^{(|I|+1) \times h}$ for items and a special token \verb|[MASK]|, where $h$ is the dimension of the model. Given $s$, the embedding look-up operation gives us $\mathbf{E}^{token} \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times h}$ :
\begin{displaymath}
  \mathbf{E}^{token} = \begin{bmatrix}
                    m_{s_1}\\
                    m_{s_2}\\
                    ...\\
                    m_{s_n}\\
                    \end{bmatrix}
\end{displaymath}
